# Growth Stock Screener Guide

A filter recipe for Fidelity's stock screener: how to find growth stocks with five-year
potential that are profitable (or close to it), generate positive cash flow, and have strong
analyst ratings.

It also explains why **P/E (Next Year's Estimate)** matters for growth stocks (§8) and includes
**sample results** from running the screen on the public Finviz screener on September 22, 2026
(§9: 92 stocks pass the core filters, 32 pass the full screen). Those results are a snapshot and
go stale quickly.
