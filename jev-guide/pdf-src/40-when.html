3When to use it

The test is simple: can you write down every acceptable answer before you ask the question? If yes, Jev is a candidate. If no, it is not.

3.1Six uses that have been demonstrated

These are not hypotheticals — each has a published implementation or measurement behind it.

UseWhat it looks like, and what was measured
Rerank retrieval results Score each candidate page for whether it actually answers the query, before any of it reaches an agent's context. On TypeSafe's legal retrieval benchmark this moved top-1 accuracy from 5% to 18% after an initial BM25 ranking, at negligible cost.
Verify agent citations Ask whether a quoted passage supports, contradicts, or simply ignores the claim made from it. A published test verified four accurate citations and caught four planted failures at 0.99 confidence.
Judge a coding agent's tool calls Before executing a command, ask whether it is irreversible, off-task, out of scope, or mismatched with stated intent. pi-warden reports 42 holds over 17,000 calls, ~88% of them correct.
Screen fetched pages for prompt injection Four yes/no questions gating whatever enters an agent's context: relevance, evidence quality, premise contradiction, injection attempt. Caught an injected instruction while preserving legitimate corrective information.
Route between models Estimate task difficulty and whether web access is needed, then send easy work to a cheap model. Vercel's AI SDK 7 exposes this directly through an evaluate method.
Classify at scale Label an entire crawled site — page type, deprecation status, presence of code examples. 500+ pages for under ten cents at list price.

3.2The pattern underneath them

Every one of those is a filter, a gate, a router, or a judge — a decision about content, made at high volume, where the answer space is small and known in advance, and where being slightly wrong occasionally is survivable because the decision is cheap enough to check or repeat.

Notice what none of them is: the thing that produces the final answer for a user. Jev sits in the layer around the model that does that.

You need a confidence number to decide whether to escalateJev — this is its distinguishing feature
