Standards referenced

CORS is defined today in the WHATWG Fetch Living Standard, and the origin concept in RFC 6454. Those documents are the normative sources; where this book and a specification disagree, the specification governs. A reading list appears at the end.

About the case studies

The real-world incidents in Chapter 15 are summarized from public disclosures, advisories and published research, and are told at the level of what went wrong and how it was fixed. Details are as reported at the time; consult the original sources before citing them.

About the lab server

The lab server is a teaching tool. It serves plain HTTP on localhost, keeps all state in memory, and must never be exposed to a network or used to protect anything real.
