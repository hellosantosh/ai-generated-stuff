 On CANCELLED

This document is written in US English, in which the prose spelling is "canceled". The enumeration member keeps the double l because it is the token the order management system and the firm's REST estate already emit, and §17.1 classifies changing an enumeration member as a breaking change. The rule that enumerations match the systems of record outranks the rule that prose matches the style guide — which is the general principle whenever the two collide.
