The tool where the gap between "returning the data" and "answering the question" is widest. A customer asking how much they can spend has one question; the account has four defensible answers, and three of them are wrong for any given account.

21.1 The problem this tool solves

Consider the same customer, the same instant, four figures:

FigureValueTrue for
cash.total18,420.00 USDNothing directly — it includes unsettled proceeds.
cash.settled12,180.00 USDWhat a cash or retirement account can actually spend today.
buyingPower.stock36,840.00 USDA margin account buying marginable equities.
buyingPower.dayTrading73,680.00 USDA pattern day trader, intraday only, resetting each session.

A tool that returns all four and stops has discharged its duty to the data and abandoned its duty to the question. The model must then know that a Roth IRA cannot borrow, that unsettled proceeds cannot fund a purchase in a cash account without a good-faith violation, and that day-trading buying power must never be quoted to someone who is not flagged. Those are three domain rules, each of which the firm already encodes correctly in its margin engine.

The fix, and the general principle

The tool returns buyingPower.applicable — the figure that actually governs this account today — alongside applicableReason, a sentence explaining why. The four underlying figures remain available for a customer who asks for the breakdown. When the server knows which of several correct answers is the right one, it must say so rather than shipping the ambiguity to a caller that cannot resolve it.

The margin account — same customer, a different correct answer, and a margin call

json The margin call is in the text rendering whether or not it was asked about

The customer asked how much cash they had. The tool told them, and also told them they owe 4,120.00 USD by Tuesday. Material adverse facts about an account belong in the primary rendering, not buried three levels into a structured payload the model may or may not traverse. This is a design rule worth writing into every tool that touches an account's financial state: if a human adviser would have to mention it, the text block mentions it.

21.6 Why this tool fails closed

brokerage_balances_get declares degradedMode: "none — fails closed rather than returning a partial figure", which is unusual in a read tool and is required by TS-OUT-11.

If the margin engine does not respond, the cash ledger alone can still produce numbers. They would be well-formed, plausible, and wrong — understating or overstating buying power by whatever the margin component contributes. A customer told they have 12,180.00 USD available when the real figure is 36,840.00 USD makes a worse decision; told the reverse, they place an order that is rejected, or worse, accepted against an incorrect assessment. The tool therefore returns DATA_INCOMPLETE and names the unavailable component:

json

The last clause of the remediation exists because of an observed failure: told only that buying power was unavailable, a model reported buying power of zero and advised the customer they could not trade. The error text now forbids both interpretations explicitly.
